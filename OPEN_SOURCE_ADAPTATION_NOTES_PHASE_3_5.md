## Local decisions

- Reserve best existing evidence by `(document, requested field)` before ordinary document/candidate caps. Supplemental section continuations compete only after field breadth. Caps and context limits remain unchanged.
- Extend `RequestedProposition` with an explicit field association. Candidate matching remains provisional; no lexical match is declared factual proof.
- Auxiliary inference keeps its existing five-second budget. Setup receives its own bounded five-second wait, and post-call cleanup/accounting a separate bounded five-second wait. A late cleanup retains its worker slot and ownership; it does not masquerade as failed inference.
- No provider swaps, additional normal LLM calls, index/schema/search-threshold changes, or licensing dependencies.
